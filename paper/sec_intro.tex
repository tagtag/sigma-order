\section{Introduction}

Recent work~\cite{sagawa2026} places a thermodynamic reading on a simple
measurement.  Take a fixed text $x$ of $L$ tokens, reverse it to obtain
$\tilde{x}$, score both with a language model, and form
\begin{equation}
\Delta \;=\; \frac{\log P(x) - \log P(\tilde{x})}{L} .
\label{eq:delta}
\end{equation}
Here $P$ is the joint distribution over token sequences that the evaluator
defines; for an autoregressive model $P(x) = \prod_k p_\theta(x_k \mid
x_{<k})$, so the joint exists and Eq.~\eqref{eq:delta} is a difference of two
of its log-probabilities.

With an autoregressive evaluator this is read as an entropy production, and a
positive value as irreversibility carried by the text.  A forward--backward
asymmetry in language has been quantified before~\cite{arrow}, but by training
a second model on the reversed corpus and comparing the two;
Eq.~\eqref{eq:delta} scores both directions under \emph{one} model, and
Ref.~\cite{sagawa2026} draws that distinction itself.  The reading is
supported by a contrast between two sets of generated passages: those whose
sentences stand in a causal sequence, and those whose sentences are arranged
arbitrarily.  The first set scores higher.

We reproduce this.  Before any of what follows, the statistical layer of the
published analysis was recomputed independently from the released per-text
values of all $\ReproNCorpora$ corpora and agrees to $\ReproMaxErr$, and every
Mann--Whitney statistic and rank-biserial effect size the repository ships is
recovered.  Nothing below disputes a measured number.

\subsection*{An open problem, and the instrument it asks for}

Reference~\cite{sagawa2026} ends by naming what its own measurement cannot
do.  The block-level production mixes genuine causal dependence with mere
temporal ordering and with whatever else the ordering carries, and separating
those contributions ``remains a highly nontrivial open problem''
\cite[Sec.~VIII]{sagawa2026}.

The difficulty is structural rather than technical.  An autoregressive model
generates in one direction, and that direction is the text's own; its
generation order and its token order are the same object.  There is no handle
by which one could be held fixed while the other varies, so no experiment
within that model class can tell the two apart.

A masked model supplies the missing handle.  It conditions on context in any
direction, so the order in which a text is assembled becomes a parameter
independent of the text itself.  Fixing an unmasking order $\pi$, the
any-order chain rule
\begin{equation}
\log P_\pi(x) \;=\; \sum_{t=1}^{L}
   \log p_\theta\!\left( x_{\pi(t)} \,\middle|\, x_{\pi(1)}, \ldots,
                         x_{\pi(t-1)} \right)
\label{eq:aoarintro}
\end{equation}
is a genuine log-probability, normalised over sequences.  Taking $\pi$ to be
the left-to-right and then the right-to-left order of one text gives the
forward path and its reverse under a single model:
\begin{equation}
\sigma(x) \;=\; \frac{1}{L}
   \bigl[ \log P_{\mathrm{l2r}}(x) - \log P_{\mathrm{r2l}}(x) \bigr].
\label{eq:sigmaintro}
\end{equation}
This is the entropy production of the stochastic-thermodynamic
definition~\cite{jarzynski1997, crooks1999, seifert2012}: one trajectory
weighed against its own reversal, not against another trajectory.

Carrying out the open problem means measuring Eq.~\eqref{eq:sigmaintro}.
Doing so settles it, though not in the direction the question anticipates,
and on the way it determines what Eq.~\eqref{eq:delta} was measuring.

\subsection*{What the instrument shows}

Equation~\eqref{eq:sigmaintro} carries an identity.  If the evaluator's
conditionals are those of some joint $P$, every term of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:aoarintro} telescopes, $\log P_\pi(x) = \log P(x)$ for every
$\pi$, and therefore
\begin{equation}
\sigma(x) = 0 \qquad \text{for every } x, \text{ causal or not.}
\label{eq:zerointro}
\end{equation}
The value range of $\sigma$ contains no property of the text.  Three
consequences follow, and they are the content of this paper.

\emph{First}, an autoregressive evaluator cannot be placed in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:sigmaintro} at all.  It has a joint, so
Eq.~\eqref{eq:zerointro} applies; and the backward term asks for
$p_\theta(x_1 \mid x_2 \ldots x_L)$, which conditions on the future and which
causal attention cannot supply.  For the evaluators of
Ref.~\cite{sagawa2026}, the quantity its thermodynamic reading names is
simultaneously trivial and unevaluable.  Equation~\eqref{eq:delta} is what
can be computed in its place --- but it compares $x$ with a \emph{different}
text, and a comparison of two states is not a comparison of a path with its
reverse (Section~\ref{sec:notareversal}).  We therefore write it $\Delta$
throughout and reserve $\sigma$ for Eq.~\eqref{eq:sigmaintro}.  The
distinction is not notational fastidiousness: the two quantities behave
differently under the very operation that defines them, and
Section~\ref{sec:blkord} exhibits a model for which $\sigma$ vanishes
identically while $\Delta$ does not.

\emph{Second}, $\Delta$ does separate the two text sets, and we ask what in
them it responds to.  Supplying the origin that Eq.~\eqref{eq:delta} lacks ---
the distribution of $\Delta$ over the $n!$ arrangements of the same text's
own sentences, against which the authored arrangement can be located --- splits
the measured distance in two: the part any writer-chosen order earns, and the
part only causal order earns.  Across the autoregressive ladder the causal
part is $\CausalShareMin\%$ to $\CausalShareMax\%$ of the total, and
it falls as the evaluator saturates --- not because causal order contributes
less, but because the room above an authored order runs out and no
normalisation can express a further increase.  The stronger the evaluator, the
less $\Delta$ can separate causal order at all.

\emph{Third}, $\sigma$ does not measure causality either, and
Eq.~\eqref{eq:zerointro} says why before any measurement is taken.  A
non-zero reading is a measure of the evaluator's \emph{in}compatibility --- its
conditionals descending from no joint --- as exposed by that text.  We measure
it on two masked evaluators across $\FbNCorpora$ corpora, both within texts
(the authored arrangement's rank among the $n!$) and between the two sets.
Both constructions place the causal contribution on the same side of zero,
and it is the side that subtracts: a causal text exposes \emph{less} of the
evaluator's incompatibility, not more.

The open problem of Ref.~\cite[Sec.~VIII]{sagawa2026} therefore has an
answer, and it is that neither quantity can do what was asked of it.  One is
not a path reversal; the other is a path reversal whose value cannot depend
on the text.  This is a stronger statement than a correction to a
measurement, and it does not rest on one: Eq.~\eqref{eq:zerointro} is an
identity, and what follows confirms rather than establishes it.

\subsection*{The labels}

One further thing is left open, of a different kind.  The two text sets were
produced by different generation prompts, and ``causal'' there means that a
language model, asked for a passage whose sentences stand in a causal
sequence, produced one.  No independent criterion is applied --- no
intervention, no counterfactual, no specified causal structure the text is
checked against.  The sets also differ in ways other than the one of
interest: in these corpora their token counts differ significantly in four of
five, and the non-causal passages are not incoherent --- their topic
continues, their pronouns resolve backwards, their connectives are in place.
They are coherent discourse whose sentence order is simply not causal.

Every measurement below that bears on causal order is therefore made
\emph{within} a text, across the arrangements of its own sentences, where
length, vocabulary, topic and style are identical by construction and the
labels enter only as a grouping of the resulting ranks.
